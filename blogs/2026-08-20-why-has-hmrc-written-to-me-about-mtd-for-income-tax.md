If you are self-employed, a sole trader, freelancer or landlord, you may have received a letter from HMRC about Making Tax Digital (MTD) for Income Tax . We’re here to explain what that’s all about, and what your next steps should be. MTD for Income Tax is the new way many sole traders and landlords will report tax.
