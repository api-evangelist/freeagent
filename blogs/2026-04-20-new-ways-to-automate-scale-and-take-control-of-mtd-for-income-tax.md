Making Tax Digital (MTD) for Income Tax is now in effect – but our mission to help practices adapt to the biggest change to UK tax in a generation is far from over. Our latest updates are designed to help practices move away from manual, client-by-client processes and towards a more automated, scalable and controlled way of managing quarterly compliance. Whether you’re managing a handful of clients or a large portfolio, these changes are built to reduce admin and give you the confidence that nothing slips through the cracks. Automate quarterly compliance FreeAgent can now prepare and submit MTD quarterly updates to HMRC automatically . These automations help to create a more consistent MTD workflow, making it easier for practices to scale their support while maintaining accuracy and control. Clients who manage their own quarterly updates can also use this feature to ‘set and forget’ and keep their MTD compliance tasks on track. Reduce practice admin and work more efficiently We know compliance is only part of the workload, so we’ve added smart automations and bulk actions to make MTD admin less painful for practices. Send client approval requests automatically We’ve drastically reduced the admin associated with the client approval process. Accountants and bookkeepers can now send quarterly updates to clients for approval automatically - and in bulk. You can also set specific tax permissions for individual clients and choose which taxes and reports to display and whether to show or hide the tax amounts due, on their Tax Timeline. So no matter how many quarterly updates you prepare, you can set FreeAgent to issue all your client approval requests on time without having to send a single manual email. File quarterly updates in bulk Our Practice Partners can now file quarterly updates for multiple clients in one go, rather than repeating the same steps for each individual client. It’s a more efficient way to work, helping you reduce manual effort, stay consistent and keep on top of deadlines - especially during busy periods. Mark multiple submissions as filed in bulk You can also mark multiple submissions as filed in a single action, replacing another time-consuming client-by-client task. This functionality is available across all three submission types: Quarterly updates End-of-year updates The final declaration It’s one more way FreeAgent helps practices cut down on admin, improve efficiency and eliminate MTD busywork.
