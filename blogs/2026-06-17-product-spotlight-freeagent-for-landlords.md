Making Tax Digital (MTD) for Income Tax is here, bringing significant changes for small business owners, but also for landlords. Starting with the first wave from April 2026, nearly one million unincorporated landlords and those managing rental income will be brought into the scope of MTD for Income Tax by 2028. We know that the real challenge of MTD isn’t just about hitting submission deadlines.
