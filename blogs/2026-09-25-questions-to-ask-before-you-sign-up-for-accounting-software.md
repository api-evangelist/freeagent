For small business owners and landlords who suddenly find themselves in need of digital accounting software to comply with Making Tax Digital (MTD) for Income Tax , it can be a headache trying to work out the right fit. It’s easy to go for the first, cheapest, or what looks like the simplest option… just to get it out of the way. With HMRC’s new way of reporting qualifying income and costs impacting millions of sole traders and landlords over the next couple of years, software providers are popping up all over the place, promising perfect solutions.
